## Style Reminders

When rewriting, ensure:

- **Clear thesis** — State the core argument early and explicitly
- **Hierarchical structure** — Use headers to organize: Problem → Conventional View → Why It Fails → Deeper Analysis → Implications
- **Specific evidence** — Support every claim with data, examples, or concrete cases
- **Address counterarguments** — Anticipate objections and show why they fail
- **Calm precision** — Maintain analytical tone with occasional dry wit
- **High information density** — Every paragraph should advance the argument

## English Expression Notes

- Use formal but readable prose
- Vary sentence length—short punchy statements for emphasis after longer explanations
- Bold key claims and turning points
- Be specific: prefer "88.3% disagreed" over "most people disagreed"
- Use logical connectors (however, therefore, this means, the key point)
