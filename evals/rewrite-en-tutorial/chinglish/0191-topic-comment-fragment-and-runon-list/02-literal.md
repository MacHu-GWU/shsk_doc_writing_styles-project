In lesson 04 you wrote a chain. But think about how you actually handle complex tasks. Something like "take this English news article, pull out 3 bullet points, translate them to Spanish, then compress the whole thing into a one-line headline." In ChatGPT's web UI your workflow looks like: ask it to extract bullets, eyeball them, copy, paste into a new conversation, ask it to translate, copy and paste again, ask it to write a headline. A three step task and your eyes and hands shuffled intermediate results around 3 times. Step 5 of AI development is automating that "human as data courier" routine. In code we call this a **sequential chain**. LCEL's `|` operator was built for exactly this: the previous step's output is the next step's input, every intermediate value stays in memory, zero copy-paste.
