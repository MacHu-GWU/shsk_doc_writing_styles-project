By lesson 04 you'd written a chain. But think about how you actually handle a complex task day to day, something like "take this English news article, pull out 3 key points, translate them into Spanish, then squeeze the result into a one line headline." In the ChatGPT web UI, that means asking it to pull the points, checking them, copying, pasting into a new conversation, asking it to translate, copying and pasting again, and asking it to write the headline. A three step task, and your eyes and hands did the shuffling three times. Step 5 of AI development is automating exactly that, the "human carries the intermediate results by hand" routine. In code we call this a **sequential chain**. LCEL's `|` operator was built for exactly this job: one step's output is the next step's input, every intermediate value stays in memory, and there's no copy-paste anywhere.
