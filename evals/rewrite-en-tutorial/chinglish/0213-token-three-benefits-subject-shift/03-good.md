- You can narrow what it's allowed to do. When you generate one, give it "read and write repositories" and nothing else: no deleting repositories, no managing the organization, no changing your account. Even if it leaks, whoever picks it up can't get far with it. It's a "keycard that opens one specific room," not a master key.
- You can revoke them one at a time, whenever you want, without disturbing the rest. Issue several tokens at once and give each one to a different program. When one of them causes trouble, you delete that one with a single click, and your other tokens and your password come through untouched.
- You can give it an expiry date. Set it to stop working automatically after 30 days, or 90. Forget to revoke it by hand and it still expires on schedule, turning itself into a worthless scrap of paper.
