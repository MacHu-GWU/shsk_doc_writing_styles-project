Here's a real case. It happened at one of the very top companies in the industry. In March 2026, even Anthropic got caught out by an oversight during packaging: they forgot to set up a .npmignore file, which does the same job as .gitignore. So they shipped about 510,000 lines of the complete source of Claude Code, one of the strongest coding agents at the time, inside a public npm package, and out it went. Within hours the code had been mirrored onto GitHub. Some repos shot past 80,000 stars and forks almost immediately, and a project derived from it hit 100,000 stars in a single day, one of the fastest-growing repos in GitHub history. It got worse. People started slipping malware into those copies to trap the onlookers who came by to download them. Once something like this happens, there's no pulling it back. Not even a world-class team can hold it down with legal action.
