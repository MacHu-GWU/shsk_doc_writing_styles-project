The 11 mini tasks below fall into eight blocks. You start with the foundations: somewhere to put things, and the ability to change them. Then comes Git's core workflow, branching and merging. The third block is a change of mindset, where everything moves onto GitHub. From there you take control of access, learning to tell public from private and to invite the right people in to collaborate. Next you open the door to automation, letting a program act on GitHub for you with a token. Then the whole routine moves onto your own machine, where you work locally with GitHub Desktop. After that you step outside your own little corner: every repository belongs to some owner, and you learn to find repositories inside an organization. The last block is a recap and a look ahead, pulling the course into a single storyline and showing you where to go next.
