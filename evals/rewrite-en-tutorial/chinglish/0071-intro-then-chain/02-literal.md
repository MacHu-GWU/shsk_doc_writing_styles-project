The 11 mini tasks below are grouped into eight parts: first lay the foundation (have a place to put things, know how to change them), then master Git's core workflow (branch and merge), then make a mental shift (put everything on GitHub), then learn to control access (tell public from private, and invite the right people in to collaborate), then open the door to automation (let a program act on GitHub on your behalf using a token), then bring the whole workflow local (work on your own computer with GitHub Desktop), then step outside your own little corner (see that every repository belongs to an owner, and learn to find repositories inside an organization), and finally recap and look ahead (connect the whole course into one storyline and see where to go next).
