Different roles put the weight in different places, too. Backend engineers get tested on all three layers, and the more senior the role, the more it leans toward layer three. For frontend and mobile engineers, layer one matters most (the object model behind state, components, and data flow), layer two is mostly about client side building blocks (local cache, push notifications, CDN), and layer three rarely goes deep on the server. Data engineers and ML engineers focus layer one on data modeling and pipeline structure, then shift layer three toward the concrete tech stack for data platforms and model serving. DevOps and SRE engineers weigh layers two and three about equally, centered on infrastructure and operations.
