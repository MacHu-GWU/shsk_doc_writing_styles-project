File thinking looks like this: a pile of files scattered around your computer, sent back and forth after every edit, nobody sure which copy is the latest or who changed what. Once the project wraps up, the pieces are strewn everywhere and you'll never round them all up again. Project thinking works differently. Every project has a home, its Repository. That's where everyone works, every change can be traced and rolled back, and the whole life cycle is right there in plain sight.
