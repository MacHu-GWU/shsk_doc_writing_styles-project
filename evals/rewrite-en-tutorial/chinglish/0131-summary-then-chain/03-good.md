Put the eight pieces end to end and they trace one continuous line of growth. You start at "I have a repo," and it isn't long before "I can change files inside it." A while later you can say "I can use branches and merges the way a professional team does," and then "all my work is banked on GitHub." From there the line runs through "I can tell what should be public from what should stay private, and I can bring the right people in to collaborate," picks up "I can let a program act on GitHub for me with a token," and reaches "I can work like a professional on my own computer with a desktop tool." By then, you can take any repository at all and know who owns it and where to go looking. Turn around at the end and you'll find you can say, "I can tie all of this into one storyline, and I can see where to go next."
